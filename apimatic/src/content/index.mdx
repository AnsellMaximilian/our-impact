Our Impact gives community-minded businesses one integration for supporter identity,
verified events, and deterministic entitlements.

Your server reports activity that it has verified. Our Impact evaluates your rules and
returns the supporter experiences that are active now. Your website can then unlock a
theme, badge, role, benefit, or any other experience you control.

## The integration boundary

The secret API key belongs only on your server. Browser integrations use a publishable
key plus a short-lived identity token minted by your backend. PayPal credentials and
payment verification stay inside Our Impact and are never exposed through this API.
